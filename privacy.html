2. 보유 기간과 파기

개인정보는 계정을 삭제할 때까지 보관하며, 계정을 삭제하면 지체 없이 파기합니다. 계정을 삭제하면 본인이 쓴 기록·일정·다이어리·사진·커뮤니티 글과 댓글이 함께 지워집니다. 가족에 본인만 있으면 아이 정보와 가족의 모든 기록도 함께 지워지고, 배우자가 함께 쓰고 있으면 아이 정보와 배우자가 쓴 기록은 남습니다. 관계 법령이 보관을 요구하는 정보는 그 기간 동안만 따로 보관합니다.

3. 제3자 제공

운영자는 이용자의 개인정보를 제3자에게 제공하지 않습니다. 다만 법령에 따라 요구받은 경우는 예외로 합니다.

BabyMoa Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-17

BabyMoa is operated by Kim Minseon (김민선). This is a summary of the Korean policy above, which prevails if the two differ.

What we collect. Account details (email, encrypted password, display name, role, nationality, language; Google account email and name if you sign in with Google); family details (region, stage, household type); your child's name, birth or due date and sex; records you enter yourself (feeding, sleep, diapers, height, weight, symptoms, medicine, solid food, contractions, vaccinations, events, diary entries, photos); community nickname, avatar, posts and comments; local marketplace listings, chats, meeting times and deal counts; push notification token and settings; the advertising ID, approximate location and app usage/diagnostics collected by Google AdMob to show ads; and the content of support inquiries.

Why. To run your account, share records within your family, show support programs for your region, remind you about vaccinations and checkups, and show ads (Google AdMob) — watching a rewarded ad to the end earns points. Your records, photos and child details are never used for ads. We do not sell your data.

Processors and overseas transfer. Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage; servers in Singapore), Google (sign-in, push delivery, AdMob ads), Expo (push delivery) and Resend (support email), each in the United States unless noted.

Retention and deletion. We keep your data until you delete your account. You can delete it in the app under Menu → Settings → Delete account, or by email as described on the account deletion page.
